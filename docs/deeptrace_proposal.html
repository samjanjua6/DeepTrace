1.1 The Problem in Pakistan: The Epidemic of Tampered Documents

Pakistani commercial banks (HBL, Meezan, UBL, Alfalah, MCB), digital wallets (Easypaisa, JazzCash, SadaPay, NayaPay), and microfinance lenders process millions of customer-submitted PDFs and scans annually. Due to accessible PDF editors and generative AI tools, financial fraud in Pakistan has reached unprecedented scale:

Major Fraud Vectors in Pakistan High Risk
  • Visa & Student Bank Statements: Widespread forgery of bank statements (HBL, Meezan, SCB) for UKVI, Schengen, and Canadian visa applications.
  • Digital Nano-Lending Defaults: Falsified salary slips, altered utility bills (LESCO/K-Electric), and doctored bank statements submitted to nano-lenders.
  • FBR Tax & Withholding Evasion: Fabricated Active Taxpayer List (ATL) proofs and falsified withholding tax deduction certificates.
  • Trade & Commercial Invoicing: Altered sales tax invoices to claim fraudulent input tax adjustments.

02 Localized Pakistani Forensic Engines

2.1 Pakistani Banking & Financial Document Topology

DeepTrace includes domain-specific forensic parsers customized for Pakistani financial documents, accounting for unique national formats, numbering conventions, and clearing architectures:

1. 24-Digit PK-IBAN & Clearing Validator Deterministic

Pakistani IBANs follow strict SBP formatting (PK + 2-digit check + 4-char Bank Code + 16-digit Account). DeepTrace executes MOD-97 checksum validation and cross-references bank clearing codes (e.g., MEZN for Meezan, HABP for HBL, UNIL for UBL, ALFH for Alfalah). 

PK36MEZN0001234567890123  --> Valid [Meezan Bank Ltd]
PK36MEZN0001234567890199  --> ❌ CHECKSUM_FAILURE (Mod-97 mismatch)
2. Lakh & Crore South Asian Numbering Parser Deterministic

Pakistani statements frequently alternate between Western comma grouping (1,250,000.00) and South Asian Lakh/Crore notation (12,50,000.00). DeepTrace normalizes and verifies all arithmetic ledgers across both formats without delimiter hallucination.

Normalization: Rs. 1,25,00,000 → 12500000.00 PKR (1.25 Crore).
3. Raast & 1LINK Narration Engine Semantic Math

Parses Pakistani transaction strings (e.g., RAAST/P2P/03001234567, IBFT/1LINK/FT26081..., ATM WDL/1LINK/KHI). Verifies chronological date sequencing, SBP transaction reference structures, and running balance continuity.

4. Pakistani Utility Bill (DISCOs/Gas) Analyzer Visual & OCR

Specialized templates for K-Electric, LESCO, IESCO, GEPCO, SNGPL, and SSGC. Cross-verifies consumer number checksums, tariff slabs, billing months, and address consistency against applicant data.

DeepTrace Proposal Section 6: Multi-Agent Forensic Investigation Unit

06 The Multi-Agent Forensic Investigation Unit

6.1 Specialized Agent Swarm Orchestration

Rather than relying on a general-purpose conversational LLM, DeepTrace deploys a specialized 4-agent swarm. Each sub-agent is strictly constrained to its domain tools and evidence buffers, eliminating hallucinations and ensuring courtroom-admissible explainability under Pakistani electronic evidence law.

Agent 1: Structural Forensic Agent Structure

Scope: Evaluates PDF object streams, metadata revisions, hidden layers, font descriptors, and creation software fingerprints.

Guardrail: Can only assert structural tampering if byte-level discrepancies or stream anomalies are verified.
Agent 2: Visual Forensic Agent Vision

Scope: Interprets ELA heatmaps, JPEG compression ghosting, copy-move clone boundaries, and visual resolution variations.

Guardrail: Formulates visual findings strictly as localized bounding box probabilities without claiming definitive legal intent.
Agent 3: Semantic & PK-Financial Agent Semantics

Scope: Verifies OCR token geometry, PK-IBAN check digits, Lakh/Crore ledger sums, Raast reference patterns, and FBR tax ratios.

Guardrail: Executes deterministic math scripts directly rather than relying on LLM mental arithmetic.
Agent 4: Lead Investigator Agent Synthesis

Scope: Receives structured findings from Agents 1–3, correlates cross-signal evidence, synthesizes the final risk explanation, and answers investigator queries.

Zero-Hallucination Policy: Forbidden from introducing facts outside the verified evidence manifest.
